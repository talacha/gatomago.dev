Notes From the Candlelit Desk

What a quiet hour, a worn notebook and a patient cat can teach you about doing the work well.

Juan Luis Villaseñor
writing as Gato Mago
The study, shortly before the second candle was lit.

The best ideas rarely arrive during the loud part of the day. They wait, politely, until the house settles and a single flame is enough to read by. This is a note about that hour, and about why I protect it.

I keep one notebook, one pen and one rule: nothing gets written down that I would not want to find again in a year. It sounds severe. In practice it is a kindness, because the page stops being a dumping ground and becomes a place worth returning to.

The three small rituals

Light the candle before opening the laptop. Write the day's single question at the top of the page. Close the book when the candle is half gone, whether or not the question has been answered.

A good question, kept warm, will eventually answer itself.

The cat, of course, supervises. He has opinions about the second ritual in particular, and expresses them by sitting on the notebook until I have chosen a better question.

question = "What is the one thing worth finishing?"
while candle.is_lit():
    work_on(question)

That is the whole method. Everything else in this grimoire is a variation on it.
